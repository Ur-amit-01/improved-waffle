NEET Notes Store ("we", "us") sells downloadable PDF study notes. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit or buy from this site, and how it's used.

Information We Collect

Since there are no accounts or logins, we collect the minimum necessary to fulfil an order and respond to enquiries:

  • Name, email, and phone number you provide at checkout via Razorpay
  • Messages you send us through the Contact form or email
  • Basic analytics data (pages visited, device type) to understand site usage

How We Use It

Information is used only to deliver your purchased notes, respond to support requests, and improve the site. We do not use your data for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Payment Data

All payments are processed by Razorpay. We never receive or store your card number, UPI PIN, or full bank details — that information stays with Razorpay and its banking partners under their own security standards.

Cookies

We use minimal cookies for essential site function and basic, privacy-respecting analytics. No cookies are used to track you across other websites.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with Razorpay (to process payment) and with email delivery tools (to send your download link), strictly for order fulfilment.

Your Rights

You can request a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, at any time by emailing us. We'll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@neetnotesstore.com or through the Contact page.
