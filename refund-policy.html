Overview

All products on NEET Notes Store are digital PDF notes delivered instantly after payment. Because of this, purchases are generally non-refundable once a download link has been issued.

Why Digital Goods Differ

Unlike physical products, a digital file can be downloaded, saved, and used the moment it's delivered — so the standard "return the item" model doesn't apply. Sample preview pages are shown on every product page precisely so you can check the format and style before buying.

Exceptions

We will issue a refund or replacement in these cases:

  • You were charged but never received a working download link
  • The PDF file is corrupted, incomplete, or won't open
  • You were accidentally charged twice for the same product

How to Request

Email support@neetnotesstore.com with your payment reference number and a short description of the issue. You can also use the Contact page.

Processing Time

Eligible refunds are processed back to your original payment method via Razorpay within 5–7 business days of approval.
