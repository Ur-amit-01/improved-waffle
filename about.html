Our Story

Notes built by people who've sat the exam.

NEET Notes Store started as a set of revision sheets shared between a study group. Three admission cycles later, it's the same notes — just typed up properly.

How It Started

From one dropper's formula sheets

The first version of these notes was a stack of hand-annotated formula sheets, passed around a NEET drop-year batch because the textbook took too long to revise from twice.

What made them worth sharing wasn't more content — it was less. Each page cut anything that wasn't actually going to show up in the exam, and marked what was, the way a topper marks their own notes before a final read-through.

That's still the only rule we build by: if it doesn't help on exam day, it doesn't make the page.
